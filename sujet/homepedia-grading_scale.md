# Defense - Homepedia

<!-- achievement -->
## Data Gathering & Sourcing

1 point per valid statement:
- Collects data from at least twelve distinct sources, and no more than fifty.
- At least one of the collected sources provides textual data (reviews, comments, free-form descriptions).
- The collected data covers the state, region, department and city levels.
- Supplements housing data with indicators from at least three other domains (economy, education, energy, environment, infrastructure, population).
- Students document and justify their sourcing choices, including their use of open or public datasets.

## Database Organization

1 point per valid statement:
- Delivers at least one relational database holding the tabular data.
- Delivers at least one non-relational database holding the non-tabular data.
- A schema of the database(s) is provided and explained.
- Data coming from different sources is standardized on common keys or indexes.
- Students explain the indexing or optimization techniques applied to their databases.

## Distributed Data Processing

1 point per valid statement:
- The processing pipeline runs on a distributed technology such as Hadoop or Spark.
- The workload is distributed across a cluster of several nodes.
- The data cleaning methodology is described and applied to the collected data.
- At least three analytical insights are extracted through the distributed pipeline.
- Students explain what the distributed setup brings compared to a single-machine run.

## Textual Data Analysis

1 point per valid statement:
- Textual data is processed, not simply mapped to its source or listed as raw comments.
- Implements a textual analysis technique such as sentiment analysis or topic extraction.
- The result of the textual analysis is exposed as a usable indicator inside the app.
- If AI algorithms are used, their implementation and functionality are documented.

## Tabular & Cartographic Visualization

1 point per valid statement:
- The app displays tables of key statistical indicators (price per square meter, price evolution, demographic or infrastructure metrics).
- The displayed tables are sortable and filterable.
- The app displays at least one cartographic representation of the data.
- Visualizations are available at the city, department and region levels.
- Students justify their chart and map types with respect to the data represented.
- Users can cross-reference at least two indicators within the same view.

## Interactive Application

1 point per valid statement:
- The app is delivered as a running interactive application.
- Users can select the geographical areas they want to explore.
- Users can select the analyses or indicators they want to display.
- The app reads its data from the project database(s), not from static exported files.
- Students demonstrate the full chain, from the raw source to the displayed visualization.
